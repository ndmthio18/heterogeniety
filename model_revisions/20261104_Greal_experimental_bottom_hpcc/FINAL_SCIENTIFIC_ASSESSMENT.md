# Final scientific assessment

Status: **not yet decidable**.

Geometry and short numerical consistency do not establish experimental validation. The final decision requires both complete 174 h cases, conservation, evaporation shape, F36 depth-wide enrichment/downward redistribution, W3 behavior, interface exchange and numerical-resolution evidence.

At present the measured saturated filter permeability and approximate inlet geometry improve experimental constraint, but unsaturated/thermal filter properties and local radial mesh convergence remain unresolved. No claim that 7.5 mm is optimal, that the filter is validated, or that MRI uniquely identifies crystallization is supported yet.
