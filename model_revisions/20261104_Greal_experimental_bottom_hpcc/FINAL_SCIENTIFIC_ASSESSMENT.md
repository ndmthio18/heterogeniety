# Final scientific assessment

Status: **pairwise decision available; complete physical validation not achieved**.

Both 174 h cases completed with maximum relative water/salt/material closure of
order `1e-12` and energy closure `1.64e-10`. Greal-81 is the defensible choice
between the two primary cases because its full-window cumulative evaporation
RMSE (0.624 g), rate RMSE (0.02476 g h-1), and final error (+1.72%) are all
lower than Greal-75 (1.781 g, 0.03529 g h-1, +8.71%). Greal-75 produces a
slightly better terminal F36 profile, but that isolated improvement does not
outweigh its evaporation and W3 penalties. Selecting it on F36 alone would be
an evidence--conclusion mismatch.

The measured saturated filter permeability and approximate inlet geometry
improve experimental constraint, but unsaturated/thermal filter properties and
local radial mesh convergence remain unresolved. The measured evaporation rate
declines more strongly than either constant-film calculation. Fixed
porosity/permeability also permits solid inventory above pore capacity.
Consequently, Greal-81 is the best of this pair, not a validated final model.
No claim that the filter is fully validated or that MRI uniquely identifies
crystallization is supported.
