# Precipitation location and detection

Both cases place all detectable solid salt in W3's top 1 mm depth bin. Top 1 mm, top 2 mm and total W3 inventories coincide within output precision. Deeper W3, F36 and filter remainders are of order 1e-36 mol or smaller, including signed roundoff, and are not physical deposits.

Greal-81 onset: 71.9167 h at 1e-12 and 1e-9 mol thresholds; 73.5833 h at 1e-6 mol. Greal-75 onset: 61.0833 h at the two smaller thresholds; 62.4167 h at 1e-6 mol.

At 174 h Greal-81 has 0.0054877985532774 mol, and Greal-75 has 0.0080813414541327 mol. These are approximately 0.320707 and 0.472274 g NaCl equivalent. Hypothetically assigning the top-bin inventory to an external crust gives approximately 0.265 and 0.390 mm at full W3 coverage, crystal density 2165 kg/m3 and crust porosity 0.43.

The evidence establishes numerical near-surface localization. It does not measure external efflorescence, coverage, connectivity or crust elevation. Filter inventory is a total-minus-sand ledger remainder rather than an independent filter-solid measurement.
# Native-field refinement

Selected ASCII VTUs at 0, 1, 2, 4, 12, 24, 60, 72, 84, 96, 108, 120, 144 and 174 h were read on HPCC. PVD times, rather than filename assumptions, select each field. All positive solid cells at threshold equivalent VF >1e-12 lie in W3's uppermost 0.0667 mm. At 174 h there are 120/138 positive W3 cells in Greal-81/75; cell counts are not area coverage fractions.
