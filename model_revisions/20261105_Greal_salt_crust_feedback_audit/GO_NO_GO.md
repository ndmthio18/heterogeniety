# Decision: NO-GO for immediate fully coupled implementation or fitting

This is an evidence-insufficiency decision, not proof that crust feedback is negligible. Surface localization and conditional resistance calculations support physical plausibility. They do not establish the operative wet/dry regime, external fraction, dimensional transport parameters, or simultaneous improvement of MRI and total evaporation. Pre-precipitation error already excludes a complete crust explanation.

Do not launch Phase II or any new 174 h case from this audit. Keep the accepted 8.100162 mm air film and existing production files unchanged.

## Concrete next-stage plan for review

1. Obtain full-text parameter definitions for the unavailable references and constrain surface wetness/contact and coverage from experiment. Do not fit these jointly to MRI.
2. Select a justified limiting regime. Ali's moist upper feature warrants explicit consideration of a connected wet layer, not automatic adoption of a dry resistance.
3. If later authorized, implement a conservative surface inventory exchange and interface energy balance as specified in COUPLED_NONISOTHERMAL_MODEL_DESIGN.md. Keep internal and external salt mutually exclusive.
4. Pass zero-crust recovery, analytical dry slab, closed salt transfer/dissolution, wet hydraulic supply and energy accounting tests before any production comparison.
5. Use bounded short comparisons with fixed air film and independently specified parameters. Evaluate both sands, reservoir solution loss, water storage, lateral salt transport and energy. A smaller MRI RMSE alone is insufficient.
6. Reconsider a long prediction only after the mechanism can affect the relevant late interval without contradicting whole-column weighing. No additional calibration freedom is authorized here.

Phase I deliverables include an explicit access limitation: no verified dimensional literature band could be produced. The decision remains conservative until that gap and the experimental regime identification are resolved.
