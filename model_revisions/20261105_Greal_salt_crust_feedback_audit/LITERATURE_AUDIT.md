## 2010WR009677 — full HTML reviewed

[Publisher full text](https://agupubs.onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/full/10.1029/2010WR009677), sections 2.3, 3.1, 4 and 5.1; paragraph numbers provide exact locations because PDF pagination was unavailable.

Columns were initially saturated, then disconnected from supply (paragraph 39); air was 30 ± 0.5°C (34). Thus sustained reservoir supply differs from G-real. Evaporation below a growing crust is represented by layered diffusion (27–31); liquid continuity to the sand does not establish continuity through the crust. Crust thickness evolves with deposited mass (31, equations 10–11). Effective crust diffusion is fitted, not independently measured: paragraph 63 explicitly gives D_crust = 0.01 D_air. Hydraulic crust permeability is not quantified in these retrieved passages. The isothermal formulation cannot replace G-real's energy balance. Heterogeneity provides bypass paths; that is not proof of wet-crust enhancement.

Equation index verified from surrounding text: (4) Raoult vapor pressure; (5) Fick flux; (6) ideal-gas concentration; (7) porous diffusion; (8) air diffusivity temperature dependence; (9) layered resistance; (10–11) concentration/thickness evolution; (13) interfacial contribution. ## Parameter and applicability decision

Only D_crust/D_air = 0.01 has been verified here as a source-specific fitted value (first paper, 30°C). Figure 3 includes that value among explicit dimensionless scenarios. Other ratios 0.05, 0.1, 0.25 and 1 are analyst sensitivities, **not a literature confidence band**. A verified dimensional band requested by task 5 remains unavailable; presenting a fabricated band would be scientifically worse than recording this limitation. Applying the fitted ratio at G-real temperatures is a hypothesis requiring validation.

Ali's direct primary evidence and its limits are documented separately. Wet-crust equations in the design report are proposed conservation/bounding relations, not attributed to these papers. Before physical parameterization, obtain the two unavailable full texts and independent constraints on coverage, wetness/contact and transport properties.
