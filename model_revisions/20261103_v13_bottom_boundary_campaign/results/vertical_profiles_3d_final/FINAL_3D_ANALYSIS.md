## Terminal port-size result

At 174 h, F36 RMSE is 0.295/0.297/0.482 mol/L for G1/G2/G3, compared with 1.227 mol/L for B0. W3 RMSE is 0.281/0.283/0.193 mol/L, compared with 0.200 mol/L for B0. Thus G1 and G2 improve F36 most but worsen W3, whereas G3 preserves the W3 match while giving a smaller F36 improvement. No port size dominates both materials.

## Interpretation boundary

B and G cases support a terminal within-3D sensitivity ranking. This does not establish grid convergence, absolute 2D–3D equivalence, or physical validation. MRI uses the archived nominal clock and is not used to infer crystallization onset.
