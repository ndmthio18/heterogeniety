## Interpretation boundary

B cases support a full-window within-3D boundary comparison. G cases do not yet support a terminal ranking. MRI uses the archived nominal clock and is not used to infer crystallization onset.

## Evidence-based interpretation

- At 174 h, increasing the B resistance from B0 to B3 reduces the F36 RMSE from 1.2270 to 0.6614 mol/L, but increases the W3 RMSE from 0.1996 to 0.3126 mol/L. This is redistribution between the two sands, not a uniform improvement.
- Relative to B0 at 174 h, B3 raises the whole-depth mean concentration by 0.5690 mol/L in F36 and 0.3679 mol/L in W3, with corresponding total-salt increases of 0.4432 and 0.2580 g. B1 is effectively indistinguishable from B0; B2 has only a small effect.
- At the latest common MRI time currently available for G1/G2 (108 h), F36 RMSE is 0.2935/0.2948 mol/L versus 0.6882 mol/L for B0, while W3 RMSE is 0.4794/0.4804 mol/L versus 0.3899 mol/L for B0. Thus the current G evidence improves the coarse-sand profile but worsens the fine-sand profile.
- G1 and G2 are nearly coincident at the plotted common times. G3 has reached only 24 h in this frozen snapshot, so no late-time G3 comparison is shown.

## Scientific claim boundary

The completed B cases demonstrate that bottom resistance can materially redistribute retained salt between F36 and W3, but no tested B case improves both sands simultaneously at 174 h. The running G cases are provisional snapshots and cannot yet establish the terminal ordering or validate the port geometry. These plots do not use MRI concentration evolution to infer crystallization onset.
