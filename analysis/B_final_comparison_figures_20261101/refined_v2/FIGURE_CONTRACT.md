# Revised figure contract

Backend: existing Python/Matplotlib workflow. Internal manuscript comparison figures; final journal production specifications remain pending. Data and scope are unchanged from the accepted final campaign package. This is a visual redesign, not a new numerical analysis or a new scientific claim.

Design: four compact quantitative grids, 183 mm wide, 156–163 mm high. Minimum intended body text 7 pt, usual tick/legend 8 pt, axis labels 9 pt. Equal final plot-area dimensions across every 2×2 grid; no free-positioned mismatched panel. Short descriptive panel titles, consistent letter anchors, white backgrounds, restrained blue/ochre/teal palette. Long methodological paragraphs move into companion captions. Every figure retains one or two short interpretation-critical notes.

1. Surface response: a time evolution and finite numerical range; b individually identified 24 h effects on a clearly labelled zoomed point axis; c buoyancy interaction across time; d three endpoint contrasts. All ten B1 scenarios and all three full 2×2 designs retained. The point-axis zoom does not replace the zero-inclusive time panel. Not a confidence interval.
2. Supply partition: a paired bottom supply for all ten scenarios; b advective/diffusive/net interface inputs for all ten scenarios; c whole-side budget and surface subset using baseline plus two distinct finite ranges; d depth bands using baseline plus numerical and parameter ranges. Replace flat category-lines with point/range plots; no deletion of variants.
3. Surface mechanism: a 0–2 mm four-term direct budget; b 0–3 mm budget with inferred lateral net; each shows the baseline, all ten scenario points and their range. c interaction contributions for baseline/fine/dt150; d baseline/fine time evolution. Asterisk remains explicit. Budget identity is not re-labelled independent validation.
4. Numerical limitations: a labelled tolerance-ratio matrix preserves all 16 selected comparisons, exact ratios and fail status; b non-monotone grid dependence; c phase/amplitude dependence of descending fraction; d onset sensitivity. All selected rows and all perturbation variants retained. No omitted failure, changed tolerance, invented regime or GCI.

Evidence source: hash-verified frozen source tables from the parent figure package. Any min–max range is calculated from the same cases as before; the case members are exported. Original figures remain available. SVG/PDF retain editable text; PNG/TIFF exported at 600 dpi. Matplotlib alignment, PDF glyph-size and collision gates precede delivery; manual visual review must assess hierarchy and readability rather than just automated PASS.
